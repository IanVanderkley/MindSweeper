# MindSweeper — Branding Design Prompt

I'm working on a solo mobile app project called MindSweeper for my Software Development program at SAIT. It's a Minesweeper game with offline Practice Mode, competitive Ranked Mode, statistics, AI Coaching, player accounts, and a leaderboard.

Before getting too far into the UI mockups, I want to establish branding that I can use consistently across the entire project. I'm looking for a main MindSweeper logo, a Master rank emblem, and a Gold II rank emblem. All three should feel like they belong in the same game, but each serves a different purpose.

I want the result to look like branding for an actual game rather than a generic school assignment. I like simple designs, but I still want the Ranked side of the game to look clean, recognizable, and slightly competitive without making it overly complicated.

## 1. Overall design direction

I'd like MindSweeper to have a dark, minimalistic visual style, mostly black and charcoal with a strong red accent. It should look modern and polished but still be easy to read and use on a mobile device.

For the default branding colours, I'd like to use:

- Main background: `#0D0D0D` (near-black).
- General UI surfaces: around `#171717`.
- Primary text: `#F5F5F5` (off-white).
- Secondary text: `#B1B1B1`.
- Borders and subtle details: around `#2B2B2B`.
- Main red: `#E83B45`, with a darker red like `#BF2333` for depth.

I'd like Roboto Condensed for the big branding text and headings, and Roboto for normal interface text. The logo text should look bold and confident rather than thin or decorative.

## 2. Main MindSweeper logo

For the main logo, I'd like something that feels connected to Minesweeper without being overly complicated.

My idea is a recognizable red mine or bomb symbol next to the MindSweeper name. I'd like to keep the symbol fairly simple. Here's what I had in mind:

- A round red centre with short spikes extending outward in several directions.
- Two shades of red to give it a little depth without making it look too complicated
- I'd like a couple of small details, such as a white square highlight and a dark angled marking
- I want the icon to be simple and recognizable as a mine, even when it's displayed at a smaller size.

For the full logo, I'd like the icon on the left and the word MindSweeper to the right, in large bold white Roboto Condensed lettering. Please make sure the capital **M** and **S** are used exactly as written.

I'd like the full logo to work as a wide horizontal graphic, I was thinking around 500–600 pixels wide and 100–200 pixels tall in its main SVG layout, with comfortable spacing and a dark rounded background. The red mark should stand out, but the text should still be the main thing you read.

The actual mine symbol is important because I want to use a simplified icon-only version elsewhere in the app, especially as the Play tab icon. I don't want the navigation icon to look like an unrelated logo. The same brand should be recognizable to players in the logo or when they are seeing the smaller symbol.

I'd rather have something distinctive and practical than an overly detailed logo with effects that disappear when it's small.

## 3. Master rank emblem

Ranked Mode is one of the main features of MindSweeper, and I want reaching Master to feel like a real achievement. Master is the highest rank in the current system.

I want a Master emblem that feels prestigious and competitive. I'm taking inspiration from the purple Ace rank badge in Rainbow Six Siege. I really like the layered shapes, purple colours, and outlines. I don't want a copy of that badge, though. I want an original design that belongs to MindSweeper.

Here's the direction I'd like:

- A tall central shield with symmetrical, pointed shapes extending out behind it on both sides.
- Dark purple as the base, medium purples through the middle, and lighter purple lines along the edges.
- A bright purple diamond-shaped centrepiece, with a highlight to make it look impressive. 
- Enough detail to feel impressive, but not so much that it becomes messy or unrecognizable in the Ranked Lobby or leaderboard.

Some colours in the direction I'm imagining are deep purple `#241E35`, violet `#553878`, lighter purple `#7256A4`, and lavender highlights around `#B79CD9` or `#E3C4FF`. The Master emblem doesn't have to use the app's red accent as its main colour; I actually like purple as the special identity for Master.

The badge should look like something a player would be excited to unlock. A layered design with good contrast is more important to me than complicated glow effects or a metallic finish.

I think a nearly square SVG would look best for the main emblem. It should still be recognizable when displayed much smaller beside player ranks, on results screens, and on the leaderboard.

## 4. Gold II rank emblem

I also want an individual emblem for Gold II, because a player's current rank is an important part of the Ranked Lobby. 

For the Gold II badge, I'd like something noticeably simpler than the Master emblem, but still polished and recognizable. I'm picturing a symmetrical, pointed shield made up of a few nested layers. It should feel like a strong mid-rank achievement rather than a cheap placeholder or a miniature copy of Master.

Here's what I have in mind:

- A shield that's wider at the top and comes to a point at the bottom.
-  A few nested shield shapes, so the outside is darker and the centre gets brighter.
- A dark bronze-brown foundation with rich gold and warm yellow highlights.
- Thin contrasting outlines around the shield layers to give it definition
- A bold Roman numeral II in the centre, so it's immediately clear that this is the second Gold division.
- A balanced, nearly symmetrical design that still reads clearly when it's small on a mobile screen.

For the colours, I'm thinking of a dark brown-gold base around `#3F2D19`, a rich inner gold around `#956222`, and a brighter gold panel around `#F0BD60`. I'd like the outer outline to be warm gold (`#FFD77B`), the inner highlight to be paler gold (`#FFF0C2`), and the numeral itself to be dark brown (`#2A1B13`) so it stands out clearly against the brighter centre. A subtle deeper-brown line around the inner panel (`#593A1D`) could help separate the layers.

I think a nearly square SVG, would look best for the emblem. It should still be recognizable when displayed much smaller beside player ranks, or on results screens.

I want this badge to represent Gold II specifically. I think Gold II is a good rank to use in my mockups because it's somewhere in the middle of the ranking system. Other ranked divisions could eventually use the same general visual system with their own colours and numerals, but I'm only asking for the Gold II emblem right now.

## 5. How I want the branding used

The main logo represents the application as a whole. It should appear wherever the MindSweeper brand belongs, such as the main Play experience and appropriate headers. The smaller red mine icon should be used for spots like the Play navigation button where the whole logo wouldn't fit.

The Master emblem represents a player's competitive rank. It should be used for Master-ranked players, the Master leaderboard, and other areas where that specific achievement is shown. I don't want it used as a replacement for the app logo or for other ranks such as Gold II.

The Gold II emblem is for players whose current rank is Gold II. It should be shown in the Ranked Lobby, player profile, and relevant statistics areas whenever that is the player's actual rank. It should not replace the main MindSweeper brand or the purple Master emblem.

I want all three assets to look like they fit naturally with the same dark interface. The regular app branding should remain mostly red, black, and white, while Master can have its own premium purple colour scheme and Gold II can use metallic gold.

The designs should remain clear against near-black backgrounds, with sensible spacing around them. Please make sure neither one becomes blurry, cluttered, or hard to recognize at normal phone-screen sizes.

## 6. Files and format

For now, I'm only asking for three finished, editable SVG branding assets.

Please use real SVG shapes, paths, and text rather than putting a raster image inside an SVG wrapper. I want to be able to resize or make small changes later without losing quality. Keep the artwork reasonably organized so it is easy to edit.

If the font is not available on another system, use a sensible fallback in the SVG. Don't package someone else's font files without the proper permission or license.

I'd like the main MindSweeper logo to have a dark rounded background, but the rank emblems should have transparent backgrounds so I can use them throughout the app. Please don't add any extra logos or rank emblems beyond the three I've requested.

## 7. Final quality check

Before calling the branding finished, I'd like you to review all three SVGs at their full size and at smaller mobile sizes.

Make sure the MindSweeper name is spelled correctly, the icon still looks like a mine, the red colours are consistent, and the logo has good spacing. For Master, check that the shield and side shapes are balanced, the centre is clearly purple rather than blue, and the emblem still looks prestigious when scaled down.

For Gold II, also check that the warm gold shield layers are balanced, that the Roman numeral II is easy to read, and that the emblem feels distinct from Master.

Please give me the actual editable SVG files, not just an image preview or a description of what you would make. I'd like to review them and request changes before we move on.

My main priority is branding that's recognizable, consistent, and appropriate for a competitive Minesweeper game, without adding unnecessary complexity.
